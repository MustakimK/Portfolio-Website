Privacy Policy – Halal Scanner

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Halal Scanner app (“the App”, “we”) handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Who we are

The App is published by Mustakim Kazi. For any privacy questions, contact mustakimkazi10@gmail.com.

What the App does

Halal Scanner lets you scan a product barcode, scan an ingredients label, or type ingredients, and it estimates whether the product is Halal, Haram, or source-dependent (“Depends”). It is guidance only, always verify with the manufacturer.

Information the App accesses

Camera. The camera is used only on your device to read barcodes and ingredient text. Camera images are processed locally and are not stored by us or uploaded to any server.

Barcode lookups (internet). When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to the Open Food Facts API to retrieve that product's name, ingredients, and image. No personal information is attached to these requests. See Open Food Facts' terms: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use

Scan history. Your recent scans (product name, status, and the product image URL) are stored only on your device. They are not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes them.

Advertising. The free version shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, including the Advertising ID, and similar data to serve and measure ads, and may share this with Google. Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), we ask for your consent first via Google's consent tools. You can remove ads with a one-time purchase or a subscription. Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases. “Remove ads” purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Data we collect ourselves

We do not operate our own servers and do not collect personal information, accounts, or analytics of our own. The only third-party data processing is by Google (ads/billing) and Open Food Facts (product lookups) as described above.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is processed only by the third parties listed above for the stated purposes.

Security

Network requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • Remove ads (and the associated Advertising ID use by AdMob) via the in-app purchase.
  • Manage your Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Clear scan history by clearing the App's data or uninstalling.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change accordingly.
